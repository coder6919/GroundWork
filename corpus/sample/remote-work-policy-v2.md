# Remote Work Policy (v2)

*Effective 2026-01-01. Supersedes the v1 policy - manager approval is no
longer required for up to 3 remote days per week.*

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements still require VP sign-off.

## Equipment

The company reimburses up to $500 for home-office equipment per calendar
year, submitted through the expense system with a receipt.

## Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable between 10am and 3pm in their local time
zone.
