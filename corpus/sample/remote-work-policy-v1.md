# Remote Work Policy (v1)

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager
approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP sign-off.

## Equipment

The company reimburses up to $300 for home-office equipment per calendar
year, submitted through the expense system with a receipt.

## Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable between 10am and 3pm in their local time
zone.
