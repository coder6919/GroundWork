New hires often ask where to find the employee handbook. It lives on the
internal wiki under Company > Policies, and is also linked from the welcome
email sent on day one.

For laptop setup, IT provisions new machines within the first 48 hours of a
start date. If you have not received a laptop invite by day two, contact
it-support@example.com rather than waiting.

Payroll runs on the last business day of each month. Direct deposit changes
must be submitted at least five business days before a pay run to take effect
in that cycle.
