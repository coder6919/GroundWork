# Time Off Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days of paid time off per month, capped at 20
days per calendar year.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit requests through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for any
absence longer than three consecutive days. Shorter absences can be requested
with 48 hours notice.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused days may carry over into the next calendar year. Any remaining
balance above that is forfeited on December 31st.
